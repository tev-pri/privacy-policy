1. Services Provided

The App provides companion mobile management services for intelligent pump uninterruptible power supply (Pump UPS) devices, including but not limited to:

  • Device Management: add, bind and unbind your Pump UPS devices, and view the list of bound devices;
  • Device Status Monitoring: view real-time operating data such as battery level, input/output voltage, load, running status and grid status;
  • Device Provisioning: connect your device to the network via Bluetooth (BLE) or Wi-Fi hotspot to complete network configuration;
  • Device Sharing: share a device with other users by phone number, and accept or send sharing invitations;
  • Notifications: receive push notifications such as device status alerts and device sharing invitations.
