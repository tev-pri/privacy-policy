When collection takes place: The App shows the Privacy Policy and User Agreement prompt when you first launch it. Before you click "Agree", we do not initialize any information-collecting SDK, initiate any network requests, or collect your personal information; clicking "Disagree" will exit the App. On the login page, you are also required to check the box agreeing to the Privacy Policy and User Agreement before you can log in. (1) System Permissions

To provide the above functions, the App may request the following permissions when you use the relevant features. You can disable or deny authorization at any time in the system settings; disabling a permission does not affect the use of functions unrelated to that permission:

  • Bluetooth Permission: used to scan for and connect Pump UPS devices (BLE provisioning and data transmission);
  • Location Permission: used only to scan for nearby Wi-Fi networks to complete device provisioning (Android requires coarse/fine location permission; iOS requires location permission while in use). We do not collect, store or upload your precise geolocation;
  • Wi-Fi Status and Network Permission: used to read/connect Wi-Fi status, complete device provisioning and network communication;
  • Notification Permission: used to receive push messages such as device sharing invitations and status alerts.

2. How We Store and Protect Your Personal Information

Retention Period: We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to achieve the collection purposes stated in this Policy. Once you request deletion or the legally required retention period expires, we will delete or anonymize the relevant personal information.

Storage Location: Your personal information is stored on servers deployed within China by the cloud services we use.

Security Measures: We take industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information, including: encrypted transmission over the network (MQTT connections use TLS encryption; cloud APIs use HTTPS); management and control of access permissions; and regular security risk assessment. We would like to be transparent with you: the App caches certain information (such as login state, device list, etc.) in plain text in the private app directory on your device. This cache is stored only on your own device and is used for in-app functions; it is not uploaded or shared with any third party. The Wi-Fi hotspot password required for device provisioning is used only for local functions.

Account Deletion: You may self-service delete your account through the "Delete Account" function in the "Settings" page of the App. Before deletion, you will be required to verify by entering your login password. After successful deletion, your account information, bound devices and their data, sharing relationships and related local cache information will be deleted or anonymized and cannot be recovered. Please proceed with caution. After deletion, you will no longer be able to log in with the original phone number or retrieve the related data. You may also sign out of your current account at any time (signing out only clears the login state on this device and does not delete cloud data). If you wish to delete your personal information by other means, you may submit a request to us through the contact information at the end of this Policy; we will verify your identity and process the deletion or anonymization promptly.
